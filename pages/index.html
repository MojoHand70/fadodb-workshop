The skeleton of FadoDB Workshop

Workshop is the open face of FadoDB: a web application for people to use, and to design, what runs on a FadoDB server. This page is its first file. It shows that the pages load under the security header they are held to, and nothing more.

What is planned

  • A use mode, for the people who work: apps, lists and documents.
  • A design mode, for a designer: classes, flows and faces, by hand or beside an agent.

The decisions made so far are in the decision records.
